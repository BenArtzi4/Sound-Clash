    Keyboard shortcuts

    Decide

    A
    Approve → next card
    R
    Reject → next card
    U
    Undo this card's decision (or the last one)
    J ↓
    Next card
    K ↑
    Previous card

    Play

    Space
    Play / pause (the video that will be used)
    1 2 3
    Play the 1st / 2nd / 3rd video on the card
    P
    Play the chosen video from the start time

    Start time

    S
    Set start = where the chosen video is now
    [ ]
    Start −1 / +1 s (with Shift: 5 s)

    Other

    /
    Search
    ?
    This help
    Esc
    Close a window / leave a field

    Every click is saved to the audit's decisions.json right away, so you can stop and continue another day. Shortcuts work on any keyboard layout (Hebrew too) and keep working after you click inside a YouTube player.
